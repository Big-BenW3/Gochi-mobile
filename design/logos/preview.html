Gochi — three logo options

Every colour is taken from mobile/src/theme/tokens.ts. Open this file directly in a browser: design/logos/preview.html

01

Signal

A single spark inside a soft aura. The most abstract of the three and the one that holds together best at tiny sizes, so it doubles as the favicon.

02

Companion

The creature itself: two ears, a dome head, one continuous visor across both eyes, a gold collar. Pairs directly with the 3D hero.

03

Core

Counter-rotating rings around a seeded core, with a transaction in orbit. Says “real onchain asset” rather than “cute mascot”.

Pick 02 if the hero leads with the creature — it is the same silhouette the 3D model will be, so the mark and the bunny agree. Pick 03 if the takeaway should be “it is actually onchain” — it reads as an asset, not a mascot. Pick 01 if you want the least committal option that will not age badly.

Check 16px honestly: that is the size that actually matters, and 03 loses its orbiting nodes there while 02 loses ear definition.
